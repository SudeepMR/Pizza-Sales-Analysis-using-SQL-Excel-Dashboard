# Pizza Sales Analysis | SQL, Excel & Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing pizza sales data using SQL and Excel to generate actionable business insights related to revenue performance, customer ordering behavior, product trends, and operational KPIs.

The objective of the project was to transform raw transactional sales data into meaningful business insights through trend analysis, KPI reporting, and interactive dashboard visualization.

The project demonstrates:
- SQL-based sales analysis
- Business KPI reporting
- Trend analysis and visualization
- Excel dashboard development
- Product performance analysis
- Customer ordering behavior analysis
- Business intelligence and storytelling

## Key Insights from the Dashboard

- The business generated approximately $817.86K in total revenue
- Average order value was approximately $38.31
- Total pizzas sold exceeded 49K units
- Friday and Saturday evenings showed the highest customer activity
- Peak order hours were between 12 PM–1 PM and after 4 PM
- Classic category pizzas contributed the highest sales and total orders
- Large-sized pizzas generated the highest sales contribution
- The Classic Deluxe Pizza and The Barbecue Chicken Pizza were among the top-performing products
- Brie Carre Pizza showed the lowest overall sales performance
